# Limitations and Non-Claims

This case study is a local reference package. It does not claim:

- production availability, latency, cost, capacity, or SLO compliance;
- enterprise-domain answer quality from a synthetic corpus;
- authorization correctness for an unconfigured enterprise IAM deployment;
- multi-agent superiority over the single-agent baseline;
- a completed AWS deployment, registry attestation, Grafana deployment, or recording;
- durability or distributed coordination for in-memory approval, state, telemetry, or
  rate-limit boundaries;
- a legal license grant.

The deterministic replay is valuable because it makes scope, provenance, and safety
contracts inspectable. It is deliberately not a substitute for load testing,
production integration, calibrated human evaluation, disaster recovery drills,
independent penetration testing, or legal review.
